# Security Incident: First 30 Minutes

This procedure coordinates application controls; it does not claim provider-edge protection is active.

## 0–5 minutes: contain and preserve

- Name an incident commander and scribe; record time, reporter, affected role/device/session and safe symptom.
- Revoke the suspected session/device or suspend the named account. Do not delete audit evidence.
- For a leaked token/credential, rotate or invalidate it through the owning system; never paste the secret into a ticket.
- For accidental public-file exposure, remove public access through the governed storage control and preserve object/version evidence.
- If finance, marks, attendance or reports may be affected, freeze the affected mutation path until authoritative state is known.

## 5–15 minutes: scope

- Review login/session, IAM, export, file-access and relevant module audit events.
- Check authorization-version changes, excessive attempts, suspicious export bounds and role/profile configuration.
- Identify affected Students/families/Staff by opaque internal references; keep personal data out of the incident channel.
- Confirm backups and logs are preserved and no recovery job is overwriting evidence.

## 15–30 minutes: stabilize and communicate

- Correct a misconfigured role only through governed IAM; revoke sessions after a material permission change.
- Lost native device: revoke device, sessions and Offline Sync authorization; preserve conflict/audit records.
- Stolen Staff password: suspend/reset through verified recovery, revoke sessions and inspect sensitive actions.
- Compromised bridge/device later: disable its mapping/ingestion authority; biometric templates are never an ERP recovery object.
- Decide safe degraded operation, owner, next update time and whether privacy/legal notification advice is required.

Do not restore, purge, rotate a shared production key, contact an external party or make a public statement without the corresponding owner authority. After containment, open the full incident record, root-cause review and recovery validation.
