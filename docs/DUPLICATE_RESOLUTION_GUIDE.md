# Duplicate Resolution Guide

1. Confirm candidate references and exact signals without exposing broad source rows.
2. Compare stable IDs, periods, linked records and authoritative evidence.
3. Treat names and contacts as supporting evidence only.
4. Choose `CREATE_NEW`, `LINK_TO_EXISTING`, `MERGE_AFTER_APPROVAL`, `KEEP_SEPARATE`, `REJECT_SOURCE_ROW` or `NEEDS_MORE_EVIDENCE`.
5. Record evidence, operator, approver, time, reason and impact.
6. Re-run dependent reference and finance checks.

Do not auto-merge siblings/Guardians sharing a phone, transliterated names, Staff with similar names, or Students with conflicting admission/DOB evidence.
