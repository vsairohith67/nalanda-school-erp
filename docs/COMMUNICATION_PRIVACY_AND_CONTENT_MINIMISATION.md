# Communication Privacy and Content Minimisation

External channels carry the smallest useful notice plus an authenticated relative application link. Prohibited external detail includes marks, full ledgers, Aadhaar/PEN/APAAR, medical/CWSN data, password/MFA/recovery material, complaint bodies, biometric punches/templates, full addresses, private attachment bytes, object keys, OCR documents and OCR candidate/extracted values.

Durable items store the authoritative subject reference, contact version, peppered destination digest and masked form. An encrypted snapshot field exists only for a future reviewed operational need; 1A resolves the current authoritative contact at dispatch and excludes encrypted/plaintext snapshots from logical backup. Destinations, provider IDs and recipient IDs are prohibited as broad metric labels. Operations views, CSV/report boundaries, errors and privacy-safe audits omit destinations and message bodies.

Native push endpoint records are user/device/environment bound and use only digest, masked display, optional encrypted snapshot, revoke state and synthetic status. Plaintext tokens and encrypted token snapshots are excluded from backup. No real token is registered.

Retention periods remain policy approval items. Intents, attempts, receipts, webhook evidence, consent/opt-out evidence, audits and dead letters can have distinct configurable schedules. Redaction may remove content where approved while retaining necessary integrity metadata; 1A does not claim provider-side deletion of delivered messages.

The public-repository scan rejects credential shapes, dialable Indian phone patterns, non-reserved email domains, database/backups, packages and private key formats across the entire changed set. Synthetic sink payload archives are not committed.
