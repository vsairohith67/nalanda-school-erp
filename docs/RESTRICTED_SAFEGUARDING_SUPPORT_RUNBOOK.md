# Restricted and safeguarding support runbook

`SAFETY_OR_BULLYING` defaults to urgent `SAFEGUARDING` and the Safety Restricted queue. Only users with the exact safeguarding permission may view it. Urgent notification goes once to the authorised leadership group and contains no complaint detail.

Confirm immediate physical safety outside the ticket system, minimise content, preserve the original statement, record factual actions, avoid automated legal/disciplinary conclusions and do not disclose internal disciplinary outcomes unless approved policy permits. Closed safety/privacy cases use restricted reopening review. No external AI or live messaging provider receives case data.
