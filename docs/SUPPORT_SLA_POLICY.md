# Support service-target policy

Acknowledgment, first response, resolution and escalation targets are versioned school configuration. They are service-improvement targets, not statutory or legal guarantees. Policies may use elapsed time or the published Academic Calendar with Asia/Kolkata office hours; missing calendar dates fall back to weekdays and the exact policy JSON is snapshotted on each request.

Only `WAITING_FOR_REQUESTER` pauses the clock, with explicit `pausedAt`, pause state and accumulated seconds. Resumption extends target timestamps append-only. Waiting for internal/school action does not pause targets. Overdue escalation is idempotent and closing cannot be used to represent an unresolved request.
