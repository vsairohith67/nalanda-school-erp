# Low Storage Runbook

1. Identify the affected approved storage category from aggregate evidence; do not expose absolute paths or filenames.
2. Pause non-essential generated-document and temporary-processing work.
3. Do not delete backups, operational databases, private uploads, audit history, or unknown files.
4. Apply documented retention only to expired metric/check/job artifacts after protected backup verification.
5. Recheck free capacity and record the result in the incident timeline.
