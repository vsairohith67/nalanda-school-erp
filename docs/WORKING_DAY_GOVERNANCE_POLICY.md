# Working-Day Governance Policy

Only an authorised Principal or leadership operator may prepare, review, approve, publish, replace or withdraw an operational academic calendar. A date has at most one classification within a version. Vacations are expanded into bounded dated rows so overlap validation and impact evidence remain exact.

Before publication the service shows working, non-working, half-day, vacation, special-working and emergency-closure totals; differences from the previous published version; affected attendance periods; existing posted attendance; and conflicts with current published examination dates. A classification that intersects posted attendance requires a specific reconciliation reason.

Publication never deletes or rewrites attendance. Attendance submission/locking records the published calendar version/day and a compact basis snapshot. Issued report-card versions likewise retain their calendar basis. A correction is a cloned replacement, preserves former history and records whether reconciliation may be required. Legal or statutory meaning is never inferred: a holiday is operational only when the school explicitly classifies and approves it.
