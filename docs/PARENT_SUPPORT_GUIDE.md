# Parent support guide

Open **Parent Support** in the active Parent role. Choose a category, add only the linked child or children relevant to the request, describe the issue and optionally attach a private PDF or still image. Fee/receipt support may reference only an issued receipt belonging to a linked child and cannot change a payment or receipt.

The timeline shows requester-visible messages, status and preserved resolutions. Internal notes are never shown or counted. A resolved request may be reopened with a reason unless its restricted workflow requires Staff review. Satisfaction feedback is optional and aggregate-only.

Urgent physical danger or medical emergencies must use the school’s immediate emergency channel; this portal is not emergency response.
