# Payroll Operator Guide

1. Confirm the payroll policy, salary-structure versions and effective dates are approved.
2. Assign compensation or create a first-of-month revision with reason and re-authentication. End payroll eligibility without deleting the assignment.
3. Create the payroll period with exact required attendance dates and input approval reference.
4. Confirm every required Staff attendance session is `LOCKED`; approve unpaid leave and manual adjustments outside payroll through their governed workflows.
5. Prepare one draft using a unique request. Calculate, inspect every formula and exception, and recalculate only while unlocked.
6. Accountant submits. Authorised leadership re-authenticates, reviews totals/exceptions, approves and locks. Locked values cannot be edited.
7. Issue private payslips exactly once. Use a new correction or reversal version for errors.

Never interpret an approval as disbursement. Do not enter Student marks, bank details, Aadhaar, PAN, UAN or passwords into payroll notes. Do not invent statutory formulas or half-day rules. Principal, Admin and Computer Operator access remains denied unless governance explicitly changes the exact permission.

For QA, use copied databases and `PAY23I`/`PAY23IQA` synthetic fixtures only. The operational zero-business baseline and real Staff records are out of scope.
