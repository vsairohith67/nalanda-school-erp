# Month-End School Management Checklist

- [ ] Student counts reconcile by active/inactive/transferred/withdrawn status, academic year, class and section.
- [ ] Guardian links show sibling families, inactive links and unlinked exceptions without cross-family visibility.
- [ ] Fee collections, family allocations, cancellations, pending dues and receipt sequence reconcile.
- [ ] Cash Book equals approved sources; expenses and miscellaneous income match their reports.
- [ ] Student attendance sessions are submitted/locked or have a named exception; Staff attendance remains separate.
- [ ] Exam/marks/report batches have explicit state, owner and publication status; no unauthorized Teacher write exists.
- [ ] Support cases, restricted cases, SLAs, reopenings and Parent Meetings have current owners/follow-ups.
- [ ] Library issued/overdue/lost/damaged items and governed charges reconcile.
- [ ] Offline devices, drafts, conflicts, rejected mutations and revocations are reviewed.
- [ ] Transport/Cafeteria/Event Media remain default-off unless a separately authorized operation exists.
- [ ] Backup verification and two-restore rehearsal are current; private-object evidence is included where required.
- [ ] System exceptions, capacity warnings, expiring sessions and feature states are reviewed.
- [ ] Record unresolved `SOFTWARE DEFECT`, `PROCESS ISSUE`, `TRAINING`, `POLICY` and `EXTERNAL` items separately.
