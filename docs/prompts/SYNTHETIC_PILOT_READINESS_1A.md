# SYNTHETIC-PILOT-READINESS-1A

Provider-independent, synthetic-only readiness phase. Do not deploy publicly, import real records, activate users, enable messaging/providers or purchase infrastructure.

Prepare role training/access matrices; office workflow; synthetic fee-day, attendance-day, month-end, report-card and reconciliation exercises; support/incident/outage drills; Offline Sync/device conflict, revocation and recovery; attachments/object recovery; escalation contacts; cutover/rollback checklists; and explicit real-data decision gates.

Require least-privilege, child/owner scope, finance corrections, duplicate prevention, backup/restore, cleanup, operational DB non-use and default-off evidence. Finish with a recommendation only; provider, DNS, real data, devices and production remain separate decisions.
