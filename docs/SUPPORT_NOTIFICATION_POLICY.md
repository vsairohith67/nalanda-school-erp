# Support notification policy

SUPPORT-1A publishes exactly-once in-app campaigns for submission, acknowledgment, assignment, requester response/information request, escalation, resolution, closure, reopening, urgent restricted alert and satisfaction request. Campaign numbers derive from event identity and are unique.

Notification copy contains no complaint text, internal note, attachment, medical/private child data, full payment reference, credential/token, storage path or raw database ID. No live Email, SMS or WhatsApp provider is activated. Future delivery providers require a separate approval and may consume only the governed event stream.
