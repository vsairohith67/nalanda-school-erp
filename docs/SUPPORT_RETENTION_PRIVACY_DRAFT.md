# Support retention and privacy draft

Status: `DRAFT_PENDING_APPROVAL`. This document has not been represented as legal advice or professionally approved policy.

Purpose is intake, routing, response, resolution, service improvement, safeguarding follow-up and accountable school operations. Collect only necessary request data. Do not collect passwords, security answers, Aadhaar, PAN, bank/payment credentials, unrelated health records, home address or unrelated Student details.

Each request stores the privacy-notice version and retention-review date and supports legal/policy hold plus archive state. This phase provides no destructive auto-purge. No retention duration is invented; leadership and qualified professional approval are required before a purge schedule or final legal wording is published.
