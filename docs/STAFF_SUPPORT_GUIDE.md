# Staff support guide

**My Support Requests** is limited to the active Staff role and the requester’s own technical, access, HR, policy and service cases. It never exposes a colleague’s request, salary, payslip, complaint or confidential note. Teacher + Parent users must deliberately switch roles; child context is unavailable in Staff support.

Payslip and salary-document requests must use **My Payslip Requests**. General support cannot issue or retrieve salary documents. Use bounded private attachments and requester-visible replies; support cases never directly alter payroll, accounts, attendance, marks, report cards, receipts or Student profiles.
