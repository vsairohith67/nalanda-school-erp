# Support lifecycle and routing policy

SUPPORT-1A is a governed case system, not unrestricted chat. Submitted requests move through `SUBMITTED`, acknowledgment, triage, assignment, work, resolution, closure and controlled reopening. Rejection, cancellation and archive are explicit terminal branches; no submitted request, message, assignment, resolution or event has a hard-delete route.

Queues are versioned configuration: Technical, Academic, Finance, Admissions, Staff/HR, General Administration, Safety Restricted and Leadership Review. Category policy controls queue, permitted assignee roles, priority, confidentiality, school-policy targets, escalation, attachment permission and linked-child requirement. Salary-document and payslip requests remain in the dedicated Payslip Requests module.

Expected-version compare-and-set protects triage, assignment, messages, transitions and resolution. Manual, urgent and target-overdue escalation is idempotent. Complaints cannot be assigned to the complained-about person, and self-assignment cannot create hidden access.
