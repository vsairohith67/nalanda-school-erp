# Synthetic School Day Runbook

Use synthetic/copied data only. Record the operator, time, environment and evidence link for every checklist. Stop on an ambiguous database target, real identity, unauthorized role or false-success message.

## Morning opening checklist

- [ ] Confirm environment banner says synthetic/test and the database path is under the approved disposable root.
- [ ] Confirm application, database, rate-limit store and private-object health; record degraded dependencies.
- [ ] Confirm backup age/status and that no restore or migration lock is active.
- [ ] Sign in as Principal and Super Admin; verify role, active session and dashboard warning state.
- [ ] Review today’s timetable, absent Staff, substitute duties and Teacher assignment gaps.
- [ ] Open Student-attendance rosters; verify active cohort, class, section and academic year.
- [ ] Review Staff attendance through the existing manual foundation. Do not wait for or simulate biometric hardware authority.
- [ ] Review Parent Meetings, support escalations, office work, offline devices and feature-state warnings.
- [ ] Confirm Offline Sync, Transport, Cafeteria, Parent Meetings and public Event Media are still production-default-off.
- [ ] Record opening Cash balance and the named Accountant who will close the day.

## Student attendance day

1. Teacher opens only an assigned class/section and verifies the roster count.
2. Record present, absent and supported late variants; inactive/transferred/withdrawn Students must not enter the active roster.
3. Submit once. Retry after a simulated network interruption and verify no duplicate session.
4. Principal locks the session. Teacher correction after lock must fail with a clear escalation path.
5. Use the authorized correction workflow; never rewrite immutable history silently.
6. Parent checks only an active linked child. A wrong child handle, inactive link and unrelated family must be denied server-side.
7. Compare class report, dashboard aggregate and Parent view.

## Teacher daily workflow

- Sign in, confirm active role and open `/teacher`.
- Review timetable and substitute work.
- Take assigned Student attendance; never broaden class scope.
- Publish classwork/homework only for assigned subjects/cohorts.
- Open exam assignments; ordinary Teacher marks-write must remain unavailable.
- View own Staff attendance/leave and assigned Parent Meetings only.
- Read notifications, preserve unsaved input on recoverable errors, then sign out.

## Parent workflow

- Select one linked child, then a sibling; verify each context switch is explicit.
- Review fees/receipts, attendance, issued reports, homework/classwork, meetings and support.
- Try wrong child, inactive link, transferred child and unpublished report handles; expect a private denial without data flash.
- Never present an offline draft, pending notification or manual UPI reference as confirmed provider delivery.

## Principal and Director

Principal owns academic and attendance exceptions, marks/report approvals, Staff operations, Parent issues and meetings. Director receives the existing bounded finance/release summaries and governance visibility. Neither role may acquire Super Admin IAM or exact full release authority through navigation.

## Super Admin

Review Command Center, My Work, Universal Search, read-only Smart AI, IAM, feature state, devices, backup, incident and system health. Smart AI has no write action. Do not enable providers, default-off features or public surfaces during this run.

## Offline office day

Only Current Year Fee, Expense and Miscellaneous Income drafts are allowed. Old Due is unavailable. Prove vault encryption, lock, queued state, restart, reconnect, one authoritative post, conflict/rejection visibility and device revoke. A local draft is never an official receipt.

## Closing

Complete the Accountant runbook, review unresolved attendance/support/meeting exceptions, confirm backup evidence, sign out all rehearsal personas and preserve only redacted evidence. Destroy disposable credentials and fixtures after the terminal evidence is sealed.
