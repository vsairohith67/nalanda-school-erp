# Employee Payroll Self-Service Guide

Open **My Payroll** while using the Staff or Teacher role context. A Teacher who also has Parent access must switch out of Parent context; Parent context intentionally shows no payroll navigation or data.

The page shows the current effective salary-structure summary, prior assignments and salary revisions, locked payroll calculations, approved attendance/leave input summaries, approved adjustments, issued payslips, and advance balance/recovery schedule. Values are explanatory salary calculations, not proof of payment.

Use **Download colour PDF** for screen use or **Printer-safe PDF** for monochrome A4 printing. Downloads are private and require the currently linked Staff account. Do not forward a payslip to public channels.

An advance request records the amount and reason for review; it does not transfer money. Payroll questions should quote the payroll month and payslip reference, never a password, identity number or bank-account detail. If the linked Staff profile is missing or wrong, contact an authorised identity administrator; payroll operators must not bypass the link.
