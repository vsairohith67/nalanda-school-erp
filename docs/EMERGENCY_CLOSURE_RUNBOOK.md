# Emergency Closure Runbook

1. Confirm the operator has active leadership context and `MANAGE_EMERGENCY_CLOSURE`.
2. Clone or create a draft operational calendar version; enter the exact affected date(s) as emergency closure and record the operational reason.
3. Inspect overlaps, current examination dates and posted-attendance impact. Obtain the required governed attendance-impact reason when applicable.
4. Submit, approve and publish or replace through the normal calendar lifecycle.
5. Verify the immutable former version, audit evidence and authorised Parent/Teacher projection.
6. Record any required attendance reconciliation separately. Never delete/rewrite posted attendance and never imply that a notification was delivered externally.

No automatic SMS, email, WhatsApp, public-site update, transport change or statutory/legal claim occurs.
