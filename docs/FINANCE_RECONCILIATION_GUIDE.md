# Finance Reconciliation Guide

Reconcile opening dues separately from historical Payments. Establish exact source control totals, count and sum in integer paise, then compare Student, family, fee-head, receipt, discount, refund, Cash Book and supported bank/cash/UPI views.

Classify differences explicitly. `MATCH` is mathematical equality. `EXPLAINED_DIFFERENCE` needs evidence and approvals. `UNEXPLAINED_DIFFERENCE`, `SOURCE_INCOMPLETE`, `SOURCE_CONFLICT` and `DO_NOT_IMPORT` block the affected batch.

Never invent a Payment to make totals agree, spread a family balance without evidence, discard a credit, or round away a difference. Preserve both source and proposed amounts in private reports.
